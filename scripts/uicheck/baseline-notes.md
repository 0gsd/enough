# `baseline.json` — what is in it, and why

Regenerated by P6c (0.3.5) from a full matrix: **59 screens × 8 viewports ×
6 languages = 2880 measurements, plus 23 scenarios × 6 languages**, with
**zero harness errors**.

**222 accepted → 45** (P6c; 35 after 0.4.1's U1, see family 2; 22 after 0.4.1's U4, see families 1 and 6). The 177
that went away were not amnestied; they were fixed or they were never real:

| what went | how many | why |
|---|---|---|
| `#help-viewer .close`, `#mini-face-btn`, `#mini-full-btn` offscreen | 64 | fixed — a CLOSED side panel now has no controls (`visibility: hidden`) |
| the paradigm / skill toggle dots and `#project-edit` at 13×12 and 15×14 | 52 | fixed — 20×20 and 22×22 hit areas, same glyphs, same row heights |
| the model modal's `×` and `#apply-model-btn` offscreen | 2 | fixed — `max-height` + an internal scroll |
| the cacheawl tile name overflowing its tile | 1 | fixed — `text-overflow: ellipsis` |
| form controls in a height-capped modal "covered by the modal foot" | ~24 | never real — `find_covered`/`find_overlaps` now honour a scroller the way `find_offscreen` always has (`geometry.scrolled_past`) |
| the composure toolbar "covered" by the readvisor panel in `rv-full` | ~30 | never real — `#readvisor-panel` joined `LAYER_SEL`; a panel that is *meant* to cover the stage is the design, like a modal |
| everything else | the rest | the P3/P4c screens they were reported under now measure the state they name (see `Driver.BOOTED`), so a dozen screens stopped reporting a blank composure's findings under a form's name |

The 45 that P6c left were **six families and two singletons**; each says who
should pick it up, and the ones that have since been picked up say so.

## 1. `sidebar-hidden` — ~~13 entries~~ **fixed in 0.4.1 (U4)**

*Was 4 covered · 8 offscreen · 1 overlap.* Fixed exactly as described below:
`.layout.sidebar-collapsed .sidebar` is now `visibility: hidden`, with the
hide delayed by the 180ms column transition (`transition: visibility 0s
linear 180ms`) so the rows still slide away, and shown at once on expand.
The screen itself also gained `SIDEBAR_SETTLED`: `wait_idle` watches the
body's size and node count, which a grid-column transition never changes, so
the screen used to be measured half-way through the slide. All 13 entries,
and the overlap in family 6, are gone; the screen measures clean in every
language at every viewport.

What it was:

One cause, and P4c-1 filed it as its known gap 9:
`.layout.sidebar-collapsed .sidebar` is a **0-width `overflow: hidden`
column whose children keep their rects**, so every tree row, every `(?)`
bubble and the skill toggle still measure as real elements — hit-testing to
whatever the canvas paints there (`#comp-title`, `#comp-viewport`, a
composure module) or falling off the bottom of the window.

**The fix is one line, and P6c already shipped the same one twice:**
`visibility: hidden` on the collapsed sidebar, exactly as `#preview` and
`#help-viewer` now do while closed. It is the sidebar's lane, not the
suite's, because the collapse animation has to be checked against it.

(`#skills-list>…>button.skill-toggle` is here because P6c grew it to 20×20;
it was in this family before at 13×12, under `tiny`.)

## 2. `project-base@ui-scale-max` — 1 entry — **the composure toolbar / uiscale lane**

*1 offscreen.*

The base view measured again at the largest display scale the viewport
allows (`uiScaleLimits().uiMax` — 1.6 at 1024×640, 2 at 1512×982). This
family was 11 entries until 0.4.1 (U1). Ten went: the composure toolbar
DOES fold (`compFitToolbar`), but it only re-measured on a window resize,
and a ui-scale step is not one — so at 200% the zoom readout, the search
field and its arrows, the fit and comments buttons and the `(?)` anchor
stayed past the right edge. The composure viewport's ResizeObserver
(auto fit) now re-applies the view on every size change, a zoom step
included, and the fold happens. The `covered` entry (`#project-edit` under
`#model-badge>span.dot`) went with the topbar rework: the project title is
an in-flow flex item now, so nothing in the bar can paint over the pencil.

What is left is `#comp-menu-btn` at 1024×640 and 160%: the composures
button is the one slot the fold never sheds, and at that size the toolbar's
fixed part alone is wider than the stage. **The toolbar needs an overflow
menu for its last slot at large scales.**

## 3. Native checkboxes and radios at 13×13 — 10 entries — **a form-control pass**

`modal-paginate` ×5 (`#pg-bring-in`, `#pg-headers`, `#pg-page-numbers`, two
`label.cv-radio>input`), `modal-ui` (`#ui-help-bubbles`), `modal-model`
(`#auto-reset-toggle`), `modal-wikisink-setup`
(`#wiki-flavor-list>label.wiki-flavor>input`), `cacheawl` (`#ca-ingest-all`),
`modal-broker` (`.broker-toggle`, 14×14).

These are **the browser's own `<input type=checkbox|radio>` at its default
size**, not anything enough draws — every one of them is 13×13 because that
is what Chrome renders. The label beside each is clickable too (they are all
inside a `<label>`), so the real target is bigger than the probe can see.

Accepted as a **class**, not one at a time: the honest fix is one styled
checkbox component used everywhere, which is a design decision and a sweep
across five modals. Until then the probe is telling the truth about the
input element and the wrong thing about the control.

## 4. `girraph` — 3 entries — **the girraph lane**

The girraph panel's two `▾` disclosure toggles (16×13) and a `→ a1`
cross-edge chip (42×15). Pre-existing, unchanged since the first baseline,
and the same treatment P6c gave the sidebar dots would fix them — but the
girraph panel's rows are denser still, so it wants its own look.

## 5. `convert-chip-*` — 6 entries — **the convert lane**

(`#convert-chip-edit>button.cc-btn` covered by `#edit-linenav` is
intermittent: some full runs report it "no longer reproduces", the next one
sees it in Japanese at 1080×1920. It stays until the chip row is fixed.)

The twin chrome joining toolbars that were already full. At 1024×640:
`#edit-revert-btn` leaves the window in German (`zurücksetzen`, 113px);
`#edit-linenav` wins the hit test over the chip's export button in Japanese;

no PDF in the scratch world — so the widths are honest but the file names
are the fixture's.

## A run-order trap the matrix fell into once (0.4.1, U4)

The full matrix reported ten NEW findings in every language but English —
`#edit-save-btn`, `#rt-heading`, the third `rt-format` button and the mini
chip's `.cc-btn` pushed off a 1024×640 window, and the dictionary's foot
catchwords cut to a few letters — and none of them reproduced in a
one-language run. Cause: the `ref-find-contents` scenario steps the scales
to ui 1.2 / text 1.3 with `applyUIScales()` (not persisted, correctly), but
then calls `rvSetState('open')`, and the panel's `rvPersist()` posted the
LIVE scales along with the panel state. So English's scenarios wrote ui 1.2
into the scratch project.json, and every later language's screens booted at
120%. Fixed at the root: `rvPersist()` now sends the scales as last
persisted (`_uiScalesSaved`), never the ones on screen. If a finding shows up
only in the languages after the first, suspect state a scenario left behind
(project.json, ui.json, localStorage, files) before suspecting the layout.

## The rule this file lives under

`baseline.json` is **a to-do list, not an amnesty**. Every key is
`(screen | class | selector-path[ | second path])` — no pixels, no viewport,
no language — so an entry survives a re-layout and **dies with the element
it names**. `--update-baseline` rewrites the file; read the diff. A key that
disappeared means something got fixed and its entry must go with it; a key
that appeared means a human has to look.

If you fix one of the families above, delete its entries in the same change.
If you add an entry, add a paragraph here saying what it is, why it is
accepted and who should fix it — an entry with no owner is an entry nobody
will ever action.
