broker

this is the broker configuration. the broker is the layer all tool calls flow through — it enforces allowlists, anonymizes off-allowlist web fetches via tor, converts fetched html to markdown, and writes a journal of every action to rness/knowledge/session-logs/<date>-broker.md. toggles below let you turn individual broker behaviors off (e.g. to skip logging overhead or block off-allowlist fetches outright). allowlist enforcement itself stays on regardless. unless you know what you're doing, leave these on.
