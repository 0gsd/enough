`comprise-oes.md` and `comprise-paradigm-map.md` are Comprise OES 1's specification and its paradigm map
(`oes/comprise-oes.md` and `oes/README.md` in comprise-dev), copied byte for byte by `scripts/sync_cosm.py`, which
records the source commit and each file's sha256 in `manifest.json` here. They are read-only in enough: never
edited here, refreshed only by the sync. enough/docoes.py enumerates them as read-only chapters of the MacroCOSM
OES doc volume, in Comprise's own words. Like the specification and the manifests (`defaults/cosm/NOTICE.md`),
they are not covered by enough's Apache License 2.0.
