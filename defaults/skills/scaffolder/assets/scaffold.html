Scaffold Loading what you wrote…

Each dashed box is a plecholder. The bracketed line is its plec: what that part has to get across. The number is a target, not a wall. Write in your own words; it saves as you type. Grey parts are fixed parts of the thing, not yours to write. A graphic box says what the picture must show and its shape; you supply the picture.

This is the whole scaffold as one markdown file, your words included. Hand it to the readvisor, or to another harness, which picks the scaffold up from it.
