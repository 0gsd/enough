**enough, briefly.**

enough is a personal language system: a place to plan, read, write, and translate text with models that run on your own machine. Nothing leaves it unless you say so.

**A project is a folder.** Anything in it opens here. Markdown and plain text open directly; PDFs, Word files, ebooks, decks, and spreadsheets open as editable markdown twins, and your edits can be written back to the original format.

**Composure is the base layer.** It is a canvas of text pages, cards, and notes for laying out what you are thinking before, or instead of, a linear draft. Everything else stacks on top of it and peels away with Esc.

**Read/edit** is for the documents themselves. Paint passages in four highlight colors and ask me about "the green parts"; I can find them and work through them with you one at a time.

**I'm your chief readvisor.** I read, plan, question, and edit alongside you, and I only touch files in ways you can see. Switch on other readvisors when you want friction or a second opinion, or seat several in a council and let them argue a question to a conclusion.

**Text planning is home.** Tell me what you want to write and we will build a plan together, then section scaffolds you fill with your own prose. Other paradigms cover translation and designing your own workflows.

**Reference is local.** The dictionary gives any word's meaning, sound, history, and relatives: right-click a word, or browse it from ui preferences. Wikisink is an offline Wikipedia. Cacheawl keeps the sources you collect.

**It grows with you.** Skills, readvisors, and paradigms are plain files you can read, change, or write yourself, and I can help you build them.

The help center, in ui preferences, has the full manual. Otherwise: tell me what you are working on.
